Behavioral RFM Framework & Strategic Playbooks
Customers clustered by Recency (R), Frequency (F), and Monetary (M) quintiles
👑 Champions R:5 | F:5 | M:5
Highest-value patrons with continuous transactions and top-tier balance holding. Primary driver of branch net interest margin.
STRATEGY: Assigned dedicated wealth relationship managers, zero-fee premium cards & FX allowances.
💎 Loyal Customers R:4-5 | F:4 | M:4
Consistent monthly account activity, salary earners, and regular debit card users. Low churn propensity.
STRATEGY: Upsell fixed-deposit tenures, mutual funds, and personalized auto/home loan credit lines.
🚀 Potential Loyalists R:4-5 | F:2-3 | M:3
Recent signups or high transaction count users with moderate average ticket size. Demonstrating acceleration.
STRATEGY: Mobile app rewards, recurring savings incentives, cashback on utility bill payments.
🌱 Single Product Users R:3-4 | F:1-2 | M:2
Account holders operating only a single product (savings or current). Prime candidates for cross-product expansion.
STRATEGY: Offer zero-deposit collateral for secondary accounts and bundled digital credit facilities.
⚠️ At Risk (Need Attention) R:2-3 | F:3-4 | M:4
Former high-velocity depositors who have dropped transaction cadence over the last 90–180 days. Risk of competitor churn.
STRATEGY: Proactive account officer outreach, survey friction points, provide reduced transfer fees.
💤 Dormant Accounts R:1 | F:1 | M:1
Zero transaction entries in over 12 months. Represents dormant balance liabilities and compliance monitoring drag.
STRATEGY: Automated WhatsApp/SMS reactivation links, frictionless digital KYC re-verification bonus.
